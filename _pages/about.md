I am a PhD candidate in Government and the Raymond Vernon Fellow at [Harvard University](https://www.gov.harvard.edu). I am also a Hans J. Morgenthau Fellow (2025–26) at the [Notre Dame Center for International Security](https://ndisc.nd.edu/), and Graduate Student Associate at the [Weatherhead Center for International Affairs](https://www.wcfia.harvard.edu/). My substantive research focuses on the dynamics of conflict and cooperation, with further works on diplomacy and crisis de-escalation. 

Before graduate school, I trained as a lawyer at the [University of Hong Kong](https://www.law.hku.hk) by day, and worked as an [open-source developer](https://github.com/CanCLID) by night.

You can use [this page](https://www.admonymous.co/jkoo) to give me anonymous feedback on my teaching, my research, or anything you think I would find helpful.
