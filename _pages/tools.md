Here are some tools I've developed over the years to make my life easier. Most of them were vibe-coded after a glass (or two) of wine - so forgive any bugs that slipped through.

## Teaching

[Grade Boundary Calculator](/tools/gradebounds): Finds the optimal grade boundaries based on student scores. Great for teachers who want the hard decisions made for them.

## Language tools

[Rime Cantonese](https://github.com/rime/rime-cantonese){:target="_blank"}: The only open-source Cantonese input method. Works on all major (and minor) operating systems - yes, even your favourite obscure Linux distro.

## Health-related stuff

[One-Rep Max Calculator](/tools/1rm): Estimate your one-rep max without risking a torn hamstring.

[Calorie Calculator](/tools/tdee): Get a personalised calorie target based on your fitness goals.

[Fat-Free Mass Index Calculator](/tools/ffmi): Quantifies how jacked you are.
